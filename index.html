THE HEART BEHIND THE WORDS

Meet Rhoda.

Okosun Rhoda is a lover of God, a nurse-midwife, and a purpose-driven individual passionate about helping people grow in their relationship with God.

She hosts The Faith Room, a podcast sharing biblical truths, real-life faith, and encouragement for spiritual growth. She also founded Rebirth Fellowship, now Edo State College of Nursing Science Christian Fellowship (EDOCNS Christian Fellowship), to nurture faith among students.

Through Called for More, Rhoda hopes to inspire you to discover your purpose in Christ and walk boldly in His calling for your life.
